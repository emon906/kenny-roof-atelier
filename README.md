# Kenny's Luxury Roofing Legacy

Create an ultra-premium **personal roofing brand website** for **Roofing With Kenny** in **Loganville, Georgia**.

TikTok: **@RooferKenny**
Trust detail: **4 Reviews**

Do NOT make it look like a typical commercial roofing company website. Make it feel personal, exclusive, cinematic, and luxury-focused.

Use only an elegant **white and warm chocolate brown** color palette with excellent text contrast so every text is easy to read.

Create a highly unique, cinematic and luxurious **hero banner**. Use a premium roofing-related background video automatically selected/generated by Lovable. Add a readable overlay, bold typography, layered elements, and subtle luxury motion.

**About Me section is required.** Make it personal, premium, and visually different from a normal About section.

Make all sections highly premium and visually unique. Use images, cinematic background videos, layered layouts, smooth parallax, reveal animations, and subtle motion where needed.

While scrolling, create impressive **scroll-based animations and video/motion backgrounds** so the website feels alive and cinematic. Different sections should reveal and animate smoothly as the mouse scrolls. Use video backgrounds where they improve the experience, but keep text readable with overlays.

Include premium sections for:

* About Me
* Roofing work/services
* Featured work/projects
* Interactive Before & After slider
* TikTok/social presence
* Trust/reviews
* Strong premium CTA and footer

Let Lovable automatically provide all relevant premium images and roofing videos.

The final website must feel like a **luxury personal brand and cinematic portfolio**, not a generic business template. Every banner and section should be visually impressive, smooth, animated, readable, and expensive-looking.
